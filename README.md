# 🍽️ Restaurant Rating Analysis
**Level 1 Data Science Project – Exploratory Data Analysis & Business Insights**

![restaurant-analysis-banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1000x300.png?text=Restaurant+Rating+EDA+Project)

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes a comprehensive dataset of **9,900+ restaurant entries across 21 features**, with a primary focus on the **Indian restaurant landscape**. The goal is to uncover rating trends, handle data inconsistencies, and provide **business-ready insights** using EDA.

---

## 🧠 Objectives
- Perform **data exploration** and **cleaning**
- Visualize **rating distributions**
- Analyze trends based on **country, city, and cuisine**
- Derive insights to aid **business decisions**

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

📊 **Rating Distribution**
- The dataset is **right-skewed**, with most restaurants rated between **2.5 and 4.0**.
- Over **2,100 entries** have a rating of **0.0**, suggesting missing or unrated data.

🌍 **Country-wise Representation**
- **India** contributes over **90%** of the data.
- Other countries (e.g., Tunisia, Greece) have insufficient entries for deep analysis.

🍛 **Cuisine Trends**
- Common cuisines include North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food.
- Combination cuisines tend to score higher ratings.

---

## 💼 Business Implications
- **High relevance** for companies operating in the **Indian food service industry**
- Useful for **restaurant platforms (e.g., Zomato)** to improve **user rating visibility**
- Not ideal for global strategy due to data imbalance
